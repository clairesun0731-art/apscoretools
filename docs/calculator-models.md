# Eight calculator launch — reviewed November 17, 2026

All eight routes reuse the existing calculator component and page structure. New calculator defaults are zero. Inputs are clamped, non-finite values become zero, results use unrounded composites, and displayed band upper limits are exclusive except for the highest band. No claim of a validated current-year score conversion is made.

## Exam structure

- Physics 1, Physics 2, Physics C Mechanics, Physics C E&M: May 2027 format, 42 MCQs (50%) plus FRQs worth 10/12/10/8 (50%). Older 35- or 40-MCQ practice papers must not be entered as if they use this format.
- Computer Science A: revised 2026 onward format, 42 MCQs (55%) plus FRQs worth 7/7/5/6 (45%).
- Computer Science Principles: through May 2027, 70 MCQs (70%), combined Create and written-response rubric total /6 (30%). No support claimed for the 2027–28 redesign.
- Microeconomics: MCQ /60 contributes 60 composite points; FRQs /10, /5, /5 contribute 30, producing /90. This is exactly two-thirds and one-third, not a 66+33=99 approximation.
- European History: MCQ /55 weighted 40%; three SAQs /3 each weighted 20%; DBQ /7 weighted 25%; LEQ /6 weighted 15%. The 2027 changes leave rubrics unchanged. Required-question instructions reflect 2027.

Each page links its College Board exam page. Physics point totals are in the linked course/exam descriptions; CSA point maxima are in its revised CED. CSP uses the six-point Create/written-response rubric in its CED. History changes: https://apcentral.collegeboard.org/courses/ap-history-exam-updates
